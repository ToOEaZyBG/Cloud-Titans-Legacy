LEGACY OF THE CLOUD TITANS - TEST

Collect your town's artifacts, assemble its legendary relic and earn a unique faction champion.

Legacy of the Cloud Titans adds an artifact-collection and hero-progression system to all nine standard towns in Heroes III ERA: Castle, Rampart, Tower, Inferno, Necropolis, Dungeon, Stronghold, Fortress and Conflux. Explore the map for components, complete faction objectives and build a matching collection that strengthens your hero and army.

WHAT YOU GET

- One universal Commander set, Ember Oath, with six parts and one assembled relic. Give an item from the backpack to the normal Commander icon; it appears in one of the six real Commander-screen slots. Right-click an equipped item for set bonuses. Shift + right-click a part with all six matching parts equipped to assemble, or the equipped relic to dismantle. No commander shop or resource cost. Bonuses count actual commander equipment only.
- Nine faction sets: eight components and one assembled relic for each town.
- 72 components and nine complete relics in total, with themed artifact artwork.
- Individual item effects and cumulative bonuses for wearing different matching pieces.
- Faction artifact quests with objective descriptions and progress tracking.
- Passive First Strike for all nine assembled faction relics.
- Nine champion quests with named heroes, custom portraits and faction specializations.
- Worn parts boost one dwelling in a linked matching town. Assembled relics boost all seven dwellings; the highest eligible bonus applies per dwelling.

FIND AND EARN COMPONENTS

Map exploration: each successful NEW random map contains two copies of each of 72 hero-set parts and six Ember Oath parts, for 156 finds in total. With underground, 78 sites are placed on each layer and each item appears on both layers. Existing eligible artifact sites are reused; additional items use free passable land next to existing adventure-map monsters, without replacing other objects or changing those armies. Complete relics never appear: assemble them from their parts. If the map cannot fit the complete plan safely, generation reports an error instead of silently claiming success. All selected sites must pass the original external-monster proximity check. No embedded armies or extra scripted battles are added; ordinary map movement and guarding rules apply.

Faction quests: open Faction set quests in your town menu. Each component has an objective and a progress counter. Defeat the listed enemies or complete the required battles, then return with the recorded qualified hero to claim it. Completed objectives stay green while that hero is away. Read the selected objective for its level and town requirements. Choose Accept with the hero in town. Only that recorded hero earns progress. Your first component or champion quest permanently locks one faction for this game. Death or dismissal resets unfinished progress; a replacement may accept for the same faction. Previously claimed component rewards remain claimed, once per player. Finding a piece elsewhere does not consume that quest reward.

Level-up rewards: with WoG option 126 enabled, eligible human Relic-category level-up rewards can offer a matching-faction component. A missing own-faction component is guaranteed at levels18,25,32,39 and every7 levels thereafter. Equipment and backpacks of all owned heroes count; assembled relics represent all eight parts. A complete collection gives an ordinary Relic instead.

Living Skull: search for a Cloud Titans component for 5 Mithril, reveal its position for 15, or pay 50 for delivery after the reveal. Bring a free backpack slot for delivery.

Custom relics are assembled from components; Dragon Utopia does not award a complete custom set. Level-up and faction-quest component rewards follow the hero's faction. A foreign-faction component found on the map is allowed.

WEAR A SET AND ASSEMBLE ITS RELIC

Different worn pieces unlock cumulative set bonuses. Components in the backpack do not count, and extra copies of the same piece do not multiply those bonuses. Item descriptions explain the effects and equipment slots.

Collect all eight different components and use the assemble/disassemble action in the faction quest menu. The assembled relic represents the full collection. Its slot depends on the relic. Disassembly returns the components to the backpack, so leave enough space.

Visit a matching owned developed town while wearing the relic to link its weekly top-tier recruitment bonus. The highest-level dwelling is required.

BACKPACK AND SET CHECKLISTS

Click Sort in the HD Backpack window. The mod locates the Backpack controls from the loaded HD provider without relying on its version number. Names are grouped alphabetically; scroll modifiers, duplicates, equipment and backpack capacity are preserved. Put down any held item first. The current reviewed profile supports artifact IDs0-551; unknown extensions are left unchanged.

Our artifact descriptions list all eight matching parts: gold means worn, green means owned but not worn, gray means missing. An assembled relic represents all eight. Only equipped pieces contribute set bonuses.

FIRST STRIKE

Wear the assembled relic to give each regular stack from its faction +25% physical damage on its first normal melee or ranged hit against the primary target. The bonus is automatic and refreshes each battle. Retaliation, secondary targets, spells, special attacks, clones and summons do not trigger it. It applies during played battles.

Tower retains the seven-piece automatic lightning effect for Giants, Titans and Lords of Thunder. The Necropolis relic retains its additional 30% physical Life Leech against living enemies.

EARN YOUR FACTION CHAMPION

Choose Champion quest in a matching owned town to enroll and view the reward and progress checklist. Enrollment is available before you meet the battle requirements.

To count the five qualifying victories, use a level-25 hero carrying four distinct matching components, or the assembled relic, and meet the town-building requirements. Commanders must be enabled. Fight with that faction's regular troops; commanders and war machines do not invalidate the army. The Grail is not required.

Hold three resource reserves: 200,000 Gold; 200 Wood and 200 Ore; 200 each of Mercury, Sulfur, Crystal and Gems. Resources and matching components are kept. The same conditions apply to all nine factions. The five ordinary qualifying faction victories remain required. There are no scripted champion trial battles.

After completing every requirement, place the bound quest hero in the matching town garrison (upper slot), leave the visitor space (lower slot) empty and select Champion quest > Receive champion. Close the town screen to complete delivery; the champion appears in that lower slot. Another hero cannot substitute. A blocked delivery keeps the reward available: move the bound hero into the garrison, free the visitor slot and keep fewer than eight heroes. Current qualification and resource reserves are rechecked at receipt. Native position, army and visitor readbacks must pass before completion. A prepared pending champion that moved elsewhere must return to the delivery town before retrying; later progression is preserved. Ctrl+J or Alt-left-click opens the checklist.

Castle: Aldric. Rampart: Sylvara. Tower: Vaelion. Inferno: Zareth. Necropolis: Andonov. Dungeon: Nyssara. Stronghold: Kargan. Fortress: Ssareth. Conflux: Aurelis.

Champions start at level 1 with 25 Attack, 25 Defense, 25 Spell Power, 25 Knowledge and a matching commander. On receipt they bring seven fully upgraded troop stacks with 500, 425, 400, 375, 350, 325 and 300 creatures. Their faction specialization works without wearing the set. Andonov has stronger faction bonuses.

STARTING A GAME

Requires Heroes III ERA, WoG, Era Erm Framework, WoG Scripts, Game Enhancement Mod and Advanced Classes Mod. Enable one copy of Legacy of the Cloud Titans - TEST and start a NEW map. Keep old saves with their matching mod version. Close the game before updating; do not use F12 to update a running game.

The current trial profile keeps Knightmare Kingdoms, the Full/Lite HD remaster packages and the other packages listed as incompatible in mod.json disabled. Support for one creature's upgrades does not establish compatibility with every creature pack.

For an online test, both players need the same build, dependencies, mod order and WoG options. Use sequential turns. Local gameplay, native AI artifact exclusion, save/load and two-computer Online Lobby acceptance are still under testing. This is an experimental release; the earlier AI-turn and fullscreen crashes have no established fix.

REPORTING A PROBLEM

Keep the save and Debug folder locally. Record the installed version, faction, exact action and a screenshot of the first error. Include whether it happened locally or through Online Lobby. Detailed test instructions are in NETWORK_TEST.md.

COMMANDER RELIC ASSEMBLY

Equip six different matching parts on the Commander, then Shift + right-click one of them and confirm assembly. The relic occupies the clicked slot, retaining full-set bonuses and leaving five slots free. Shift + right-click the equipped relic to dismantle. Right-click an equipped item to see bonuses and progress. Existing original items and battle growth are preserved; parts without a free commander slot go into the backpack. Green N/6 headings and part checklists show active progress; two matching parts retain their2/6bonus. Complete relics never drop. There is no commander purchase menu or resource cost. See COMMANDER_SETS.md.

COMMANDER PARTS AFTER BATTLE

Every confirmed human victory has a5%chance to discover one random commander part. Only the six Ember Oath parts can appear; complete relics and AI rewards are excluded. Ordinary battle loot and guaranteed hero-set level rewards are retained. A full backpack cannot receive the part and consumes that attempt without rerolling.
